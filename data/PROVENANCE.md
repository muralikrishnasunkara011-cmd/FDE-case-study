# Dataset provenance and permitted use

All nine records are **synthetic transcript fixtures**. No audio, video, authentic NHA catalogue data, or verified historical recording is included. Timestamps are deliberately constructed test clocks generated by `scripts/make_fixtures.py`; they are not alignments or estimates for real recordings. The production ingestion path never generates timing from text length.

The Churchill passage reproduces the short quotation supplied in the customer's assessment. The event title and date (4 June 1940) come from that case study. This assessment use is not a claim that the speech or an audio recording is globally public domain. Obtain appropriate clearance before broader redistribution of historical materials. All remaining passage prose is original synthetic test content, supplied for unrestricted use with this PoC. No claim is made about an original recording of the event.

Anchors used in results identify the version names in the table. All custody references are fictitious DEMO-SHELF values. `online` describes the availability fixture, not playable media. The model is `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, revision `c9745ed1d9f207416be6d2e6f8de32d1f16199bf`, 384 dimensions. Its model card describes an Apache-2.0 licence: https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2. Model weights are downloaded separately and are not redistributed in this repository.

Fixture judgements are authored alongside the corpus and form a development/contract evaluation, not an independent blind relevance study. No ASR accuracy can be measured without audio and reviewed reference transcripts.
